Beef Brised in Red wine

A classic Boeuf Bourguignon features beef braised in red wine. For a quick, at-a-glance guide, the essential ingredients are:The Meat: \(1\text{ kg}\) (2 lbs) beef chuck roast (cut into 2-inch chunks).The Liquids: 1 bottle (750 ml) full-bodied red wine (like Pinot Noir) and \(500\text{ ml}\) (2 cups) beef stock.The Aromatics: \(150\text{ g}\) bacon, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 3 garlic cloves, and a herb bundle (thyme, parsley, bay leaf).The Garnish: \(200\text{ g}\) pearl onions and \(200\text{ g}\) mushrooms, sautéed in butter.

vegetable coconut sambar

A Vegetable coconut sambar is prepared with toor dal, mixed vegetables such as carrot, beans, potato, drumstick, brinjal and tomato, along with grated coconut, sambar powder, cumin seeds, dry red chillies, turmeric powder, tamarind, salt and oil. For tempering, mustard seeds, cumin seeds, dry red chillies, curry leaves and asafoetida are use.

square deviled eggs

Deviled eggs ingredients: boiled eggs, mayonnaise, mustard, salt, black pepper, paprika, and finely chopped spring onions or chives. Optional ingredients include pickle relish, hot sauce, or a little lemon juice.

vegetable rice

Vegetable rice ingredients: rice, mixed vegetables such as carrot, beans, peas, potato and capsicum, onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chillies, oil, salt, and spices such as cumin seeds, garam masala and turmeric powder. You can also add coriander leaves for garnishing.
