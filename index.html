Beef Brised in Red wine

A classic Boeuf Bourguignon features beef braised in red wine. For a quick, at-a-glance guide, the essential ingredients are:The Meat: \(1\text{ kg}\) (2 lbs) beef chuck roast (cut into 2-inch chunks).The Liquids: 1 bottle (750 ml) full-bodied red wine (like Pinot Noir) and \(500\text{ ml}\) (2 cups) beef stock.The Aromatics: \(150\text{ g}\) bacon, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 3 garlic cloves, and a herb bundle (thyme, parsley, bay leaf).The Garnish: \(200\text{ g}\) pearl onions and \(200\text{ g}\) mushrooms, sautéed in butter.
